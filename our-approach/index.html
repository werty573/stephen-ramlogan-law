Our approach

What working with the firm looks like, from the first phone call to the close of your matter.

Legal problems are stressful enough without uncertainty about the process. This page sets out what you can expect when you bring a matter to the firm.

From first call to resolution

  1. Your first call

    The office takes your name, a contact number and a short description of the matter, then offers you an appointment. If the matter is urgent, such as an arrest or a court date this week, say so.

  2. A conflict check

    Before advising, the firm confirms it is not already acting for anyone on the other side. This is why you may be asked for the names of other parties.

  3. The consultation

    A private meeting to go through the facts and your documents. You will leave knowing where you stand, what the options are, and what you would need to decide.

  4. Instructions and fees

    If you decide to proceed, the scope of the work and how fees will be charged are confirmed with you before work starts.

  5. Working on your matter

    Letters, documents and court steps are explained before they happen. You receive copies of important documents and updates when something changes.

  6. Closing your file

    When the matter ends, you are told the outcome, what it means for you, and where your original documents are kept.

Bring to your first meeting

  • A valid form of identification
  • Every document connected to the matter, even ones that seem minor
  • A short timeline of events with dates
  • Names and contact details of the other people involved
  • A list of the questions you want answered

Confidentiality

What you tell your attorney for the purpose of legal advice is confidential and protected by legal professional privilege. Staff at the firm are bound by the same duty.

Please use the consultation form only for a brief description. Detailed or sensitive information is best shared in person.
