Practice areas

Five areas of practice, each covering the matters clients in South Trinidad most often bring to an attorney.

Civil Litigation

Disputes over contracts, money owed, damage to property and personal injury, from the first letter to trial.

  • Breach of contract
  • Recovery of money owed
  • Personal injury claims
  • Motor vehicle accident claims
Civil Litigation

Criminal Law

Advice at the police station, bail applications and representation in the Magistrates' Court and the High Court.

  • Advice during police questioning
  • Bail applications
  • Summary offences in the Magistrates' Court
  • Indictable matters in the High Court
Criminal Law

Family Law

Divorce, custody and access, maintenance and the division of matrimonial property, handled with discretion.

  • Divorce and separation
  • Custody and access to children
  • Child and spousal maintenance
  • Division of matrimonial property
Family Law

Land & Conveyancing

Buying and selling property, deeds of conveyance and gift, mortgages, title searches and land disputes.

  • Sale and purchase of land and houses
  • Deeds of conveyance
  • Deeds of gift between family members
  • Mortgages and discharges
Land & Conveyancing

Wills & Estates

Wills, powers of attorney, probate and letters of administration, explained in plain language.

  • Preparing and updating wills
  • Powers of attorney
  • Grants of probate
  • Letters of administration
Wills & Estates
