What to expect when you contact the firm

Our approach

  1. Call or write

    Tell the office briefly what the matter concerns. You will be offered an appointment.

  2. Meet in confidence

    A private first meeting to hear the facts, look at your documents and answer your questions.

  3. A clear plan and fee

    You are told the options, the likely next steps and how fees will be charged, before you commit.

  4. Kept informed

    Your matter moves forward with regular updates and plain explanations at every stage.

Fees, explained before you commit.

Many conveyancing, will and document matters can be quoted as a fixed fee. Court matters are usually charged in stages. Either way, you will know how fees work before work begins.

Fees & consultations

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Consultations are by appointment so that time can be set aside for your matter. Call the office or send a consultation request.

Is what I tell the firm confidential?

Yes. Communications with your attorney for the purpose of legal advice are confidential and protected by legal professional privilege.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Identification and any documents connected to the matter: letters, contracts, deeds, court papers or photographs. A short written timeline helps too.
