Fees & consultations

How fees are charged, what affects the cost of a matter, and which charges are paid to the State.

Every matter is different, so the firm does not publish a single price list. What it does promise is clarity: before work begins you will know how fees will be charged and roughly what to expect.

Fixed fees

Often available for defined work such as wills, powers of attorney, deeds of gift and many sale or purchase transactions.

Typical for: Wills & Estates, Land & Conveyancing

Staged fees

Court matters are usually charged stage by stage, so you know the cost of the next step before it is taken.

Typical for: Civil, Criminal and Family matters

Government fees

Stamp duty, registration fees and court filing fees are paid to the State. They are separate from legal fees and shown separately.

What affects the cost of a matter

  • How complex the facts and the law are
  • Whether the other side contests the matter
  • How many court hearings are needed
  • The value of the property or claim involved
  • Urgency and the time available
  • The documents and searches required

If you cannot afford an attorney, you may qualify for help from the Legal Aid and Advisory Authority of Trinidad and Tobago, which assists eligible people in certain criminal, family and civil matters.
