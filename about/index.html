About the firm

A San Fernando practice, established in 2019, built on personal attention and plain explanations.

A practice that knows its clients by name.

Stephen Ramlogan & Co. practises from Nos. 16-18 Gordon Street in San Fernando. The firm was established in 2019 and acts for individuals, families and small businesses across South Trinidad.

The practice covers the matters people most often need an attorney for: disputes that need resolving, criminal charges, separation and children, buying and transferring land, and planning for and administering estates.

As a smaller firm, it can give each matter direct attention. You will know who is handling your file, how to reach the office and what is happening next.
